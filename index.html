The person behind the page

Curious about words.
Grounded in people.

I'm Hassan, a BA English student who's happiest when I'm either on a cricket pitch, on a basketball court, or helping young kids learn something new. Studying English has taught me to pay attention to how people express themselves, and teaching has shown me how rewarding it is to pass that on. This site is where I keep track of what I'm learning and building.

For me, learning is not only about what is written on a page. It is also about listening, sharing ideas, and being willing to try again.
